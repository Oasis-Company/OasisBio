# Getting Started with OasisBio

Welcome to OasisBio! This guide will walk you through creating your first digital identity.

## Prerequisites

- A web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- An email address for registration

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Visit the OasisBio homepage
2. Click **"Get Started"** or **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your email for a verification code
5. Enter the verification code to activate your account

## Step 2: Create Your First Identity

1. From your dashboard, click **"Create New Identity"**
2. Fill in the basic information:
   - **Character Name**: Choose a name for your identity
   - **Tagline**: A brief one-line description
   - **Identity Mode**: Select the type that best fits
3. Optionally add an era (time period)
4. Review and save

## Step 3: Explore the Editor

After saving, you'll be taken to the editor where you can:

- **Identity Tab**: Edit basic information
- **Eras Tab**: Add multiple time periods
- **Abilities Tab**: Define skills and capabilities
- **Worlds Tab**: Create fictional universes
- **DCOS Tab**: Write decision-making scripts
- **References Tab**: Add external resources
- **Nuwa Tab**: Use AI to deepen your character

## Step 4: Publish Your Identity

1. Click **"Publish"** in the editor
2. Choose a unique URL slug
3. Your identity becomes publicly discoverable
