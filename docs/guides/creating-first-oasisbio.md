# Creating Your First OasisBio

This guide provides an in-depth walkthrough of creating a detailed and compelling digital identity.

## Understanding Identity Modes

Choose the right identity mode for your character:

| Mode | Description | Use Case |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Real** | Your actual self | Personal profiles, professional identities |
| **Fictional** | Completely fictional characters | RPG characters, story protagonists |
| **Hybrid** | Mix of real and fictional | Personal brands with creative elements |
| **Future** | Your future self | Goal setting, career planning |
| **Alternate** | Parallel universe versions | What-if scenarios |
| **Worldbound** | Bound to specific worlds | Characters existing only in fictional universes |

## Building Your Identity

### 1. Basic Information

Start with core details:
- **Name**: The primary identifier
- **Tagline**: One-line hook
- **Summary**: Brief overview (2-3 sentences)
- **Description**: Detailed narrative

### 2. Adding Eras

Eras represent different time periods in your character's life:

```
Era: "The Gilded Age"
Type: Past
Duration: 1870-1900

Era: "Present Day"
Type: Present
Duration: 2024-Present

Era: "Year 2150"
Type: Future
Duration: 2150-2180
```

### 3. Defining Abilities

Abilities are skills and capabilities:

- **Technology**: Programming, engineering
- **Combat**: Martial arts, weapons
- **Social**: Persuasion, leadership
- **Knowledge**: Languages, research
- **Magic**: Supernatural abilities
- **Survival**: Wilderness skills

### 4. Creating Worlds

Worlds are fictional universes your character inhabits:

- **Summary**: Brief overview
- **Time Setting**: When the world exists
- **Geography**: Locations and terrain
- **Social Structure**: Governments, factions
- **Rules**: Physics, magic systems
- **Major Conflicts**: Ongoing tensions

### 5. Writing DCOS Scripts

DCOS (Decision Choice Outcome Script) defines how your character makes decisions:

```markdown
## Decision Pattern: Ethical Dilemmas

When faced with moral choices:
1. Consider the greater good
2. Weigh personal principles
3. Choose the path of least harm

## Decision Pattern: Risk Assessment

When evaluating danger:
1. Gather information
2. Calculate odds
3. Prepare contingencies
```

### 6. Adding References

References are external resources:

- Books that influenced the character
- Historical figures
- Media inspiration
- Real-world locations

## Best Practices

1. **Be Consistent**: Maintain continuity across eras
2. **Be Specific**: Concrete details are better than vague ones
3. **Show Growth**: Characters should evolve across eras
4. **Limit Scope**: Focus on what matters to your story
5. **Use Nuwa**: Let AI suggest deeper connections
