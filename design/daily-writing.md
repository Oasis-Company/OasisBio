# Daily Writing — The Lightweight Loop

## The Goal

To make "adding to your identity universe" feel like **opening a notebook**, not **filling out a form**.

The daily writing loop is the retention engine of OasisBio.
It doesn't need to be impressive. It needs to be **effortless and cumulative**.

---

## Two Entry Modes

### Mode 1 — Daily Question (Guided)

The system presents one question. The user answers. Done.

**Properties**:
- One question per day (not a list, not a form)
- Question fits in one line
- Answer can be one sentence or twenty paragraphs — both are valid
- The question is contextualized: "Yesterday you wrote about your childhood city. What do you miss most about a place you've left?"
- Estimated time: **2 minutes**

**When to suggest this mode**:
- New users (first 30 days)
- Users returning after a gap
- Users who haven't answered a Proust question in 7+ days

---

### Mode 2 — Free Writing (Unguided)

The user opens a blank doc and writes whatever they want.
No prompt. No format. No structure.

**Properties**:
- Lands directly in DCOS (their document archive)
- Auto-saves as draft
- Title is optional (defaults to today's date)
- Can be tagged retroactively
- Estimated time: **whatever they want**

**When to suggest this mode**:
- Users with established habits (30+ days)
- Users who have used Mode 1 at least 10 times
- Users who have manually opened DCOS before

---

## The Accumulation Principle

Neither mode should feel like a discrete task to "complete."

**The mental model we want**: A river, not a bucket.
Writing a little every day doesn't "fill" OasisBio — it **flows** through it,
depositing layers that become visible only over time.

Design implications:
- No progress bars ("You're 43% done with your identity")
- No streak counters (they create anxiety, not meaning)
- No completion states ("Your profile is complete!")
- **Do show**: "Your earliest entry is from [date]. You've added [N] pieces since then."

---

## How Writing Becomes Identity

Every piece of writing in the daily loop feeds the larger system:

| Writing Type | Where It Goes | How It Builds Depth |
|-------------|---------------|---------------------|
| Proust answer | DCOS `/proust/` folder | Thematic clusters emerge over time |
| Daily question answer | DCOS `/daily/` folder | Time-series of self-reflection |
| Free writing | DCOS (user-organized) | Raw material for Era, World, and Ability structure |
| Tagged entry | EntityTag system | Becomes discoverable within the identity universe |

Over time, the system can surface:
> "You've mentioned 'the future' 23 times. Want to shape what that future looks like? → Create a Future Era."

---

## The Invitation Architecture

The daily writing loop should feel like receiving a letter, not doing homework.

**Copy principles**:
- Empty state: *"What's on your mind today? It doesn't need to be profound."* (not "Start writing your entry")
- Saved state: *"Added to your universe."* (not "Entry saved successfully")
- Return prompt: *"It's been a while. Your last entry was [N] days ago. No rush — what's changed?"*
- Depth prompt: *"You've been writing for [N] days. Something is taking shape. Want to see it?"*

---

## What This Loop Is Not

- Not a social media post (it's private by default)
- Not a journaling app (it feeds a structured identity system)
- Not an AI conversation (the user writes; AI reads later, via Nuwa)
- Not a task ("Write your entry for today") — it's an invitation, always optional
